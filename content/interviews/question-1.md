# Tell me about a product you improved

## What the interviewer is testing

- Whether you can identify a meaningful product problem
- How you use evidence instead of only opinion
- How you worked with others to create impact
- Whether you can explain outcomes clearly

## Strong Answer Structure

| Step | What to Cover |
| --- | --- |
| Context | Product, users, and business situation |
| Problem | What was not working and how you knew |
| Action | What you changed and how you aligned the team |
| Result | Metric movement, learning, or business impact |

## Sample Answer Outline

Use a STAR format. Start with the situation, explain the task, describe your actions, and close with measurable results.

---

## Practice Prompt

Write a 90-second version of your answer. Keep one metric, one customer insight, and one tradeoff.

# What metrics would you track after launching a new feature?

## What the interviewer is testing

- Outcome orientation
- Ability to avoid vanity metrics
- Understanding of guardrail metrics

## Strong Answer Structure

| Metric Type | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Adoption | Are users trying it? |
| Engagement | Are users getting repeated value? |
| Outcome | Did the business or user result improve? |
| Quality | Did errors, complaints, or latency increase? |

## Sample Answer Outline

I define success before launch, choose one primary outcome metric, add adoption and engagement diagnostics, and monitor guardrails so a local improvement does not harm the broader product.

## Practice Prompt

Choose one feature and define one primary metric plus two guardrails.

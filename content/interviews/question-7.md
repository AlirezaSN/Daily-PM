# How would you decide whether to build, buy, or partner?

## What the interviewer is testing

- Strategic thinking
- Cost and speed tradeoffs
- Understanding of long-term product control

## Strong Answer Structure

| Option | Best When | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Build | Capability is core and differentiated | Slower delivery |
| Buy | Need speed for a standard capability | Vendor dependence |
| Partner | Need market access or specialized expertise | Alignment complexity |

## Sample Answer Outline

I would evaluate strategic importance, urgency, internal capability, total cost, integration complexity, compliance needs, and long-term control.

## Practice Prompt

Use a fraud detection feature as an example and compare build, buy, and partner.

# How do you prioritize features?

## What the interviewer is testing

- Your ability to connect priorities to strategy
- Whether you understand opportunity cost
- How you handle uncertainty and stakeholder pressure

## Strong Answer Structure

| Dimension | Example Questions |
| --- | --- |
| Goal | What outcome are we trying to improve? |
| Impact | Which users or business metric will change? |
| Confidence | What evidence supports the idea? |
| Effort | What is the cost and complexity? |
| Risk | What could go wrong? |

## Sample Answer Outline

I start from the product goal, compare opportunities using impact, confidence, effort, and risk, then discuss tradeoffs with design, engineering, and business stakeholders.

## Practice Prompt

Pick three features from a product you know and rank them. Explain why the second-best option is not first.

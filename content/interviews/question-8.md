# Design a product for freelancers to manage invoices

## What the interviewer is testing

- Product sense
- User segmentation
- Problem framing before solutioning

## Strong Answer Structure

| Phase | What to Do |
| --- | --- |
| Clarify | User type, geography, payment methods, pain level |
| Discover | Current workflow and failure points |
| Define MVP | Invoice creation, reminders, payment status |
| Measure | Time saved, paid-on-time rate, repeat usage |

## Sample Answer Outline

I would focus on freelancers who invoice multiple clients monthly, identify where payment collection breaks, and design the MVP around fast invoice creation, reminders, and clear payment tracking.

## Practice Prompt

List three assumptions you would test before designing screens.

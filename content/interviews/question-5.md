# Tell me about a conflict with engineering or design

## What the interviewer is testing

- Collaboration style
- Respect for technical and design constraints
- Ability to resolve conflict without authority

## Strong Answer Structure

| Step | What to Show |
| --- | --- |
| Shared goal | Start from user or business outcome |
| Difference | Explain the disagreement fairly |
| Evidence | Bring data, customer context, or constraints |
| Resolution | Show the decision and relationship outcome |

## Sample Answer Outline

A strong answer does not make the other function look wrong. It shows how you made the problem clearer and helped the team choose together.

## Practice Prompt

Write one example where you changed your mind because another function had better evidence.
